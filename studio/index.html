About

Led by Santhosh.

ARQ. is a design studio founded by Santhosh Muthu, a UI developer and UX/UI designer based in Birmingham. He holds an MSc in User Experience Design from Birmingham City University and has worked across marketing, product design and 3D visualisation.

He designs from research first, builds what he designs, and shows the working along the way. If you want to see how he thinks, the case studies and the daily series are public.
